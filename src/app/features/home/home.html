About Me

I’m a yoga instructor devoted to sharing practices that invite deep rest, self-inquiry, and sustainable transformation. Strings of Yog is rooted in the understanding that we are all connected—through breath, awareness, and subtle inner threads that guide us home to ourselves. My teaching blends traditional yogic wisdom with modern nervous-system awareness, creating practices that are accessible, inclusive, and deeply nourishing. Whether you are new to yoga or a seasoned practitioner, you are welcome here exactly as you are.
